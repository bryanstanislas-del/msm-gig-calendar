**Title:** Festival Hub 2027

**Intro**

Festival Hub is Music Scene Magazine's free guide to UK festival application
windows for grassroots bands and solo artists. Every listing is checked
against the festival's own website, so you can see who's taking applications
for 2027, when they close, and where to apply.

[Button, same tab] **Open Festival Hub** → `https://festivals.musicscenemagazine.co.uk/`

**What's in it**

- A real directory of **26 UK festivals**. When we checked their official
  sources on 27 October 2026, **13 had applications open** for 2027. The
  others were closed, not yet open, not yet announced, or couldn't be
  confirmed. Each listing shows its status and the date we last checked it.
- Deadlines in UK time. Where a festival gives only a date, the Hub says the
  closing time isn't known, so apply early.
- Filters by region, genre, deadline and status.

**How it works**

1. **Find festivals that fit.** Read each festival's brief: eligibility, location,
   genre, set length, and whether covers are accepted.
2. **Apply directly to the festival.** The Hub links to each festival's own
   application page. MSM never submits applications for you, and you never
   need an MSM account to apply.
3. **Keep a shortlist.** Save festivals and track your progress: stage, outcome
   and a short checklist. Your shortlist is stored only in your own browser, on
   that device. Nothing is sent to MSM, and no contact details are collected.
4. **Check the dates again before you apply.** Statuses can change at any time,
   and "Open" only means the festival's page showed an open 2027 call when we
   checked. Always confirm on the festival's own site.

**Please note**

- **MSM does not book festivals and cannot get you a slot.** Each festival
  makes its own decisions.
- **Check pay and costs before you apply.** Many calls don't say whether
  performers are paid, and some slots are unpaid or expenses-only. Some
  festivals charge an application fee. Work out your travel and time costs,
  ask the festival if anything is unclear, and be wary of any request to pay
  to perform.
- MSM hasn't tested the festivals' own application forms.

**Get your act on the MSM Gig Calendar**

A free artist profile on the MSM Gig Calendar gives festivals somewhere to see
your live dates. It's optional and never needed to apply.
